PLSLEND Charts

A free, open community chart room: the PulseChain and HEX pairs next to the macro series that move them, with the tools people miss most when a paid platform pulls the ladder up — trend lines, a price-range ruler, trend-based Fibonacci extensions, SuperTrend, RSI and MACD. No account, no tracking, no cookies.

Your drawings

Drawings are saved in this browser only (localStorage, nothing leaves your device). Clearing site data deletes them, so use export now and then; import restores them on any device.

Keyboard

Esc cancel / cursor · Del delete selected drawing · Shift + / Shift − zoom · ← → scroll

Data

    Macro and TradFi series are refreshed twice a day by an automated job; on-chain pairs load live from GeckoTerminal (with a cached copy as fallback). Weekly and monthly bars are built from the native data; timeframes a series cannot support are greyed out.

    Everything here is for information only and is not financial advice. Open source: github.com/PLSLEND/charts. Chart engine: KLineChart (Apache-2.0).
